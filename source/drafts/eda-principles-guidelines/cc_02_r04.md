### EDA-R-04: Queues have a defined expiration period

| Strategy | Queues have a defined expiration period. |

<details><summary>

Open section for explanation, rationale and exception conditions 

</summary>


#### Description

Unconsumed events should be automatically deleted after a defined retention period to prevent indefinite message accumulation. AMQP queues are configured with an expiration period appropriate to the business context and processing requirements.

### Label
SHOULD

#### Rationale

*	EDA-P-10

#### Implication

*   Messages that remain unconsumed beyond the configured retention period are automatically deleted, potentially resulting in loss of unprocessed events.
*	Consumers must ensure timely processing of events to avoid unintended data loss.
*	Monitoring and alerting may be required to detect queues approaching message expiration thresholds.
*	Queue configuration management enforces the expiration policy consistently across environments.
