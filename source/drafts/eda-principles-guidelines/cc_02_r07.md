### EDA-R-06: EDA-R-07: Producers use publisher confirms to ensure message persistence

| Strategy | Producers use publisher confirms to ensure message persistence. |

<details><summary>

Open section for explanation, rationale and exception conditions 

</summary>

#### Description

Event producers must use synchronous publisher confirmations (AMQP _publisher confirms_ mode) to ensure the broker has durably persisted each message before it is considered sent. This guarantees that no events are silently lost in transit and enforces at-least-once delivery semantics.

Messages must be published to durable queues so they survive broker restarts. The producer must handle negative acknowledgments or timeouts by retrying the message or raising an alert.

#### Label

MUST

#### Rationale

*	EDA-P-08
*	EDA-P-09

#### Implication

*   Producers must enable _confirmSelect()_ on AMQP channels.
*	Messages must be marked persistent and sent to durable queues.
*	A producer must not consider an event delivered until the broker confirms persistence.
