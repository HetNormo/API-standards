Guidelines translate the principles into concrete implementation rules and recommended practices. They specify how to apply the principles in day-to-day design and engineering, ensuring clarity and promoting consistent, interoperable implementations across teams. While principles establish the why and what - the strategic intent and desired outcomes - guidelines provide the how of daily development work.

**Guideline format**
Each guideline follows a structured format:
*	**ID**: Unique identifier (EDA-R-XX)
*	**Guideline**: Short statement of the requirement or recommendation
*	**Label**: Conformance level (MUST, SHOULD, MAY)
*	**Description**: Detailed explanation and scope of the guideline
*	**Rationale**: References to underlying principles (EDA-P-XX)
*	**Implications**: Practical consequences for implementation

**Relationship to principles**
Guidelines directly support the architectural principles defined in section 2.1. The rationale section explicitly links each guideline to its supporting principles, ensuring every implementation rule traces back to strategic architectural decisions. This connection helps teams understand not just what they must do, but why it matters.

As event-driven systems in the energy sector mature, these guidelines will evolve based on practical experience, while the underlying principles remain stable anchors for decision-making
