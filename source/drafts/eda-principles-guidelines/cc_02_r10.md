### EDA-R-10: CloudEvent content is encoded in the AMQP DATA section

| Strategy | CloudEvent content is encoded in the AMQP DATA section. |

<details><summary>

Open section for explanation, rationale and exception conditions 

</summary>

#### Description

When CloudEvents are transported over AMQP, the event content must be encoded in the AMQP DATA section of the message body.

The DATA section is the standard AMQP mechanism for carrying opaque binary payloads, for example as UTF-8 encoded JSON and provides a consistent representation for CloudEvents across implementations.

CloudEvent metadata may be mapped onto the AMQP metadata as an additional action.

#### Label

MUST

#### Rationale

*	EDA-P-08
*	EDA-P-12

#### Implication

*	Producers must place CloudEvent content in the AMQP DATA section
*	Consumers must read and deserialize CloudEvent content from the AMQP DATA section
*	Implementations must not use the AMQP VALUE section for transporting CloudEvent content, as this can lead to interoperability issues between platforms and client libraries

</details>
